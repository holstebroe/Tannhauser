# 06 — User interface

Software-rendered panel in the Acidus/Gritbaal style: no GUI libraries, procedural drawing
into a 2× supersampled ARGB buffer, box-filtered to the window (`src/gui/`). Shading helpers
and anti-aliased atlas fonts come from Acidus `gui/modern/` (`ModernDraw`, `LabelFont`,
`DisplayFont`, regenerated with `tools/gen_gui_fonts.py`). X11 and Win32 window glue as in
Acidus (macOS Cocoa is a stub, plan WP5 issue).

## 1. Look

The CS-80 front panel [S §4, §5; photo `docs/reference/cs80.jpg`]: black/charcoal panel,
white silk-screen lettering, colour-coded slider caps, walnut side cheeks, red-brown felt
strip, reversed "paddle" performance levers.

Programming-row caps as on the hardware photos: **white** PWM speed, noise, VCF level, sine,
level, touch level; **green** PWM, PW, HPF, LPF, touch brilliance; **red** resonance;
**black** IL, AL, envelope A/D/S and the waveform rockers; **yellow** the R sliders and the
sustain time. Performance paddles keep grey/green/white groups.

**Printing** follows the CS-80 photos: in the programming rows the name is under each slider
(`RES` with a subscript H/L, `VCF LEVEL`, waveform symbols ⎍ ⟋ ∼ for the square/saw rockers
and the sine slider) and the scale legends are at the slot ends: FAST/SLOW (PWM speed), 10/0,
90%/50% (PW), HIGH/LOW (filters), −5/0 (IL), +5/0 (AL), LONG/SHORT (times), ON over the rockers.
Middle strip: KEYBOARD CONTROL prints BRILLIANCE and LEVEL over LOW/HIGH pairs; TOUCH RESPONSE
prints PITCHBEND / INITIAL and SUB-OSC AFTER under SPEED/VCO/VCF; ring modulator ATTACK, DECAY,
DEPTH, SPEED, MODULATION; DETUNE II, MIX (I top, II bottom), BRILLIANCE, RESONANCE. Left-hand
panel: Sustain I/II and Portamento/Glissando rockers with their legends, FOOT SW., and the
sustain and portamento times as sliders marked LONG (top) / SHORT, as on the hardware. Names
too wide for their column are shrunk to fit.

## 3. Control types and interaction

| Type | Used for | Interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slider (programming) | per-line sliders, sustain and portamento time | vertical drag; shift = fine; double-click = default; wheel |
| Paddle (performance) | middle strip, chorus/reverb | as on the hardware the paddle grows when pulled **down** (toward the player); drag down = more, wheel down = more |
| Rocker | Square, Saw, Chorus, Tremolo, Sustain I/II, Porta/Gliss, sustain pedal | click toggles |
| Lever selector | Feet (6), Sub Func (6) | click a position / drag / wheel |
| Knob | Volume, Pitch | vertical drag |
| Tone button | 2 × 14 tone selector | load factory tone into that line; Memory slots (doc 05 §6) |
| Preset LCD + arrows | preset browser | click → menu; arrows step; wheel steps |
| Ribbon | ribbon | drag horizontally: pitch relative to first touch, release → 0 |
| Keyboard | 61 keys (MIDI 36–96) | click/drag to play (velocity from vertical click position) |

Every value change goes through `TannhauserClap::onBegin/onParamValue/onEndEditFromGui`
(host gestures). The GUI polls parameter values at 30 fps and redraws only controls whose
value changed (per-control dirty rectangles over a cached static panel layer).

A readout at the right of the header shows `name: value` (CLAP value-to-text) of the control
under the mouse or being dragged, or a hint for tone buttons, the display and the keyboard.

Rendering (`GuiWindow.cpp`): three 2× buffers — `static_` (panel print, drawn once),
`compose_` (static + controls; a control is redrawn only when its visual state changes, after
restoring its bounds from `static_`), `display_` (compose + menu and readout overlays) — then a
2×2 box filter into the window's pixels for the dirty rectangle only. Each control type has
fixed margins (`margins()`) that must contain everything it draws.

## 4. Menus

A single popup-menu widget (dark, green LCD text) with one level of submenus: preset browser
(categories → presets, multi-column when long), Save Preset As…, Rescan User Presets, Init
Patch. Hovering a category opens its submenu; closed by a click outside, right-click or Escape.
